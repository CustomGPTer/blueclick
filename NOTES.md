BlueClick 1.0.0, the first release.

- Your phone as a Bluetooth remote for TVs, computers, tablets and phones: TV remote, trackpad, air mouse, keyboard with voice typing, slide clicker, camera shutter, media keys and gamepad.
- A preset for each kind of device, many saved devices, your own buttons and macros, and any button reprogrammed for one device.
- Clicker and controls widgets, a quick settings tile, and the phone's volume buttons working with the screen locked.

Bluetooth only: it works with devices that accept a Bluetooth keyboard or mouse, and has no infrared. Android 11 or later. Check the file against SHA256SUMS before installing.
