Privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026. This policy covers the BlueClick app for Android (package uk.jj.blueclick) and this website. BlueClick is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who can be reached by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.

The short version

  • BlueClick has no internet permission. It cannot go online, and nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.
  • It talks only to the devices you pair it with, over Bluetooth.
  • There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Voice typing is turned into text on the phone, and nothing is recorded.

What stays on your phone

BlueClick keeps these in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • The devices you have paired with it, with their names, presets, layouts and any buttons you changed.
  • Your own buttons and macros.
  • Your settings.

Android's cloud backup and device transfer are switched off for BlueClick, so none of this is copied to Google.

What goes to your devices

When you press a button, BlueClick sends the key, mouse movement or media key to the paired device over Bluetooth, exactly as a Bluetooth keyboard or mouse does. Text you type or speak is sent to that device as key presses. BlueClick does not keep a record of what you type.

Voice typing

Voice typing uses Android's speech recogniser on the phone itself. BlueClick asks for the microphone only when you first use it, listens only while the microphone button is on, and never saves or sends the sound.

Backup files

A backup is made only when you ask for one, and saved where you pick. It holds your devices, buttons, macros and settings, and BlueClick never sends it anywhere.

Keeping and deleting your data

Remove a device in BlueClick to forget it and its buttons. Uninstalling BlueClick deletes everything it has kept.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands on your phone.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no scripts. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement.
