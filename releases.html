Release notes

What changed in each version of BlueClick. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.0 27 November 2026Latest

  • First release. Your phone as a Bluetooth remote for TVs, computers, tablets and phones.
  • Presets for Android TV, other smart TVs, Windows, Mac, presentations, camera, media, keyboard and mouse, gamepad and your own phone's audio.
  • TV remote, trackpad, air mouse, swipe pad, keyboard with UK, US and Mac layouts, and voice typing on the phone.
  • Slide clicker with a talk timer, black or white screen and laser pointer, and the phone's volume buttons working with the screen locked.
  • Camera shutter with single shots, bursts, time-lapse and timers.
  • Your own buttons and macros for each device, and any button reprogrammed for one device.
  • Clicker and controls widgets, a quick settings tile and notification controls.
  • Many saved devices, one-handed and left-handed layouts, large buttons, dark and light themes, reduced motion and backup to a file.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.0 is called BlueClick.apk. It is 3.0 MB (2,983,419 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

61d0826e98d69c51751db6c05a2f0c4932d753868a52075378a84cccce613a1f

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile BlueClick.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
